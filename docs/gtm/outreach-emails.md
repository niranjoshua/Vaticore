# Outreach templates

Keep every message short, specific, and about the operator's cost, not our technology. One clear ask per message. Replace the bracketed parts. No attachments on the first email; a link to the site is enough.

---

## 1. Cold email (to a Head of Operations or Head of Data)

**Subject:** forecasting to cut diesel at [Operator]'s sites

Hi [Name],

I build short-term load and solar forecasts for distributed energy operators. The aim is narrow and measurable: help you hold the right battery reserve and schedule the genset deliberately, so you burn less diesel and serve more demand.

It is probabilistic, so you see the likely case and the bad case, and it is built for sparse, messy meter data rather than clean grid data.

I am running a small number of free pilots. If you can share six to twelve months of history for one site, I will forecast its load and solar and show you the result measured against your current baseline, in litres and cycles, not error metrics.

Worth a 20-minute call to see if it fits [Operator]?

[Your name]
[link to site]

## 2. Warm intro follow-up (after a mutual contact connects you)

**Subject:** thanks for the intro, [Contact]

Hi [Name],

[Contact] suggested we speak. Short version: I forecast load and solar for mini-grid and C&I solar operators and turn it into a battery and genset advisory, to cut fuel and unserved energy.

I would love to run a free pilot on one of your sites: you share history, I forecast it and score it against your own baseline, you keep your data and we touch nothing live.

Are you free for 20 minutes [two specific time options]?

[Your name]

---

## 3. Discovery-call agenda (send before the call)

**Subject:** quick agenda for our call

Thanks for the time. I will keep it to 20 minutes:

1. How you forecast load and solar today, and where it hurts (diesel, battery, unserved demand).
2. What site data you have, at what resolution, and who owns it.
3. Whether a free shadow-mode pilot on one site is worth trying, and what success would look like in your terms.

No slides. I want to understand your sites, not pitch at you.

---

## 4. Post-call follow-up with the pilot offer

**Subject:** pilot proposal for [Operator], one site

Hi [Name],

Good talking. As discussed, here is what I propose:

- One site, six to twelve months of load and generation history.
- I forecast it, backtest against your baseline, and report diesel, battery, and unserved-energy impact for that site.
- Free, about two to three weeks from data to report, advisory only, you keep your data.

I have attached the one-page pilot proposal and a mutual NDA. If the NDA works, send the data checklist back and I will start the week it lands.

[Your name]
