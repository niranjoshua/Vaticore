# Your data, and the trust boundary

Operators act on a forecast only if they trust it and trust us with their data. This page states, in plain language, what we do and what we will never do. It is written to be read by an operations lead, not a lawyer, and it should match whatever we sign.

---

## You own your data

- Your metering and generation data stays yours. Sharing it with us for a pilot grants no ownership to us.
- We use it only to forecast your sites and to show you the result. Nothing else.
- We do not sell it, share it with other operators, or use it to train models that serve your competitors, unless you give written permission.
- When a pilot ends, we delete your raw data on request and keep only what you agree we can keep.

## Advisory only, never control

- Vaticore produces forecasts and recommendations. That is all.
- We do not connect to, command, or automate your dispatch, your battery, or your generator.
- Every output is a suggestion a human acts on, or ignores. The decision stays with your team.
- This is a deliberate safety and liability boundary, not a missing feature. We will not cross it early, and never without an explicit, separate agreement.

## Honest evaluation

- Every forecast is scored against a naive baseline on your own history. If we do not beat "same as yesterday," we say so.
- We report accuracy and estimated impact with their assumptions shown, so you can check our work.
- Estimated savings are labelled as estimates until a live pilot measures the real number on your sites.

## Security basics

- We sign a mutual NDA before you send anything.
- We prefer secured links over email attachments for data transfer.
- We scope out any column you flag as sensitive.
- Secrets and operator data never live in our code or public repositories.

## In one sentence

You keep your data, we only forecast, a human always decides, and we prove our numbers against your own baseline.
