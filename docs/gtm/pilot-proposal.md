# Vaticore shadow-mode pilot

**A free, fixed-scope forecasting pilot for one of your sites. You keep your data. We touch nothing.**

---

## The promise

We forecast your site's electricity load and solar generation, day ahead and rolling intraday, and turn each forecast into a battery-reserve and generator advisory. We measure the result against your current practice and report it in your terms: litres of diesel, battery cycles, and kWh of unserved energy, not machine-learning jargon.

If our forecast does not beat what you do today, we tell you so plainly. That is the point of a pilot.

## Why forecasting, and why now

Every dispatch decision at a hybrid site is a bet on tomorrow's load and sun: how much battery to hold back, when to lean on the genset, how much demand to expect. When that bet is wrong you burn diesel you did not need, cycle the battery for no reason, or leave customers in the dark. Better forecasts shrink all three.

Vaticore is probabilistic. It gives you a range (P10 to P90), not a single guess, because a battery sized for the average day fails on the worst one.

## What we do in the pilot

1. You share six to twelve months of historical load and generation for one site (see the separate data checklist).
2. We ingest it, forecast load and solar, and backtest against a naive baseline on your own history.
3. We produce a short report: forecast accuracy, and an estimate of diesel, battery, and unserved-energy impact for that site.
4. We review it with you and agree whether to move to a live shadow-mode run alongside your existing process.

## What we do not do

- We do not connect to or control your dispatch. Advisory only. No closed-loop control.
- We do not share, resell, or train shared models on your data without written permission.
- We do not need live system access for the first pilot. Historical files are enough.

## What we ask

- One site with roughly six to twelve months of load and generation history.
- One operations or data contact for two or three short calls.
- A success metric agreed in writing before we start, in your currency (for example, "reduce estimated diesel buffer at Site X by a measurable amount").

- **Your effort:** a few hours, mostly exporting data and two review calls.
